# thread0 0.1.360 (Windows and macOS)

This release lets you point at what to change in a result — an image, a PDF, a web page or a running web app — and send that straight back to the session that made it. Signing out of a PC now tells your phones and guests why they were disconnected, organization PCs can no longer slip out of their policies by switching accounts, and hosts get a guest activity log they can actually read. Notifications and account emails now come in your language.

## Changes

### Edit a result and send it back
- Open a result (image, PDF, HTML page) or a running web-app preview and switch from **View** to **Edit**. Mark a spot with a point, box, circle, line or freehand stroke, type what should change, and press **Edit now**. The instruction goes to the session that made the file, together with the file's location on the PC, a picture of your marks and exact coordinates.
- PDFs keep marks per page and include the text under each mark. Slide decks and other HTML that changes as you click can be stepped through and marked screen by screen. A running web app is drawn on the PC at the page you are looking at, signed in as you.
- Several images from one message open together, with a page list on the side.
- If the session is busy, the edit waits and is sent when it finishes (or now, if you choose). If the session was closed — for example after the PC app restarted — **Edit now** reopens it and sends there.
- Works the same in the PC app and on the phone. On the phone, results show as compact rows with one ⋯ menu (View, Edit, Share…), and the instruction box always stays on screen.
- The PDF viewer fits the page to the screen, with zoom and page controls in a small floating bar.

### Results
- Each turn's files are split into the main results and "working files", which are folded away so the list stays short. The result panel has a search box.
- "Library" is now **All files**: every result from every session on the PC, plus the files kept on this device.

### Signing out and switching accounts on a PC
- Phones and guests connected to a PC belong to the thread0 account that connected them. If you switch the PC to another account, they are paused (not deleted) and reconnect without pairing again when you switch back.
- Signing out asks first and shows what will stop (for example "phones: 2 · guests: 1"). Your phones then say "thread0 signed out on Office PC" with the time, instead of "the PC is off", and guests see the same reason on their screen.

### Organizations
- On a PC enrolled in an organization, signing out removes the PC from the organization first, and the organization records it. If the organization cannot be reached, nothing changes and you are told why.
- If the enrolling account's session simply ends, or another account signs in, the PC stays enrolled with its policies in force (shown as "Paused" in Settings) instead of quietly leaving. Administrators can release such a PC from the console.
- The admin console shows each PC with its state and history, and the audit log reads as sentences grouped by member, day and session, with important events highlighted.

### Guests
- The guest activity log shows waiting requests first (with Allow and Decline), a summary for today, the last 7 days or all time, and the guest's work grouped into short sentences by day and session. The raw record is still one click away.

### Language, notifications and email
- Push and system notifications are written in the language of the device that receives them.
- Password-reset, email-verification and invitation emails share one thread0 design in nine languages and come from thread0.ai.
- Korean text on Android phones uses a web font, so word spacing shows correctly.
- Google sign-in always lets you choose the account.

### Fixes
- Switching a session from GPT to Claude no longer fails with "Prompt is too long" on very long GPT sessions; only what GPT itself would resume is carried over.
- GPT sessions reopened right after closing no longer fail to start.
