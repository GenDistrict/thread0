# thread0 0.1.378

Windows and macOS (Apple Silicon) ship the same version.

A much faster thread0 Remote on slow connections, simpler PC-to-PC connections, clearer sign-in, and fixes for Auto mode, GPT and translations.

## Changes

### thread0 Remote (phone and the Remote tab)
- Replies show up right away. On a slow mobile connection the first reply used to appear up to about 17 seconds after the PC sent it; now it follows within a fraction of a second, and long answers keep updating while they are written.
- Sending a message no longer downloads the whole conversation again. The phone keeps what it already has and asks the PC only for what changed — about 1 KB instead of hundreds of KB per message.
- Opening a session from a notification is fast: the end of the conversation appears within a second or two, even for a session you have never opened on that phone, and the rest fills in above without moving what you are reading.
- If thread0 Remote was closed, tapping a notification now opens that session directly instead of the home screen.
- Your phone keeps a copy of recent conversations so it can show them before the PC answers. Only on your own devices, never in guest mode, and it is removed when you sign out, disconnect the PC or delete the session.
- Very long answers are fetched in pieces as they grow instead of from the beginning each time.
- The phone checks that its copy of the conversation matches the PC after every update and fixes itself if it ever does not.
- Remote opens on the last home screen it saw while it connects to the PC.
- Photos attached for the AI are resized on the phone first (2048 px on the long edge), so they upload faster.
- On a stuck connection screen, **Send diagnostics** finishes within 15 seconds; if it cannot send, the same button saves the file instead.

### Your PCs
- Settings → Connected devices has one **Connect my PCs** switch on this PC's row. Connected PCs open each other in Remote without asking for approval, and a phone you approve on one of them works on all of them.
- When you turn it on, the code appears only after the chosen PC has actually received the request. If that PC is off or thread0 is closed there, you are told so within 10 seconds instead of being shown a code that cannot work.
- Turning it off tells your other PCs.

### Sign-in and first start
- The Claude sign-in card is one line — sign in in your browser and press **Authorize** — with the full steps under **Show details**.
- While waiting for a sign-in, thread0 now says in large type: finish signing in in your browser and you will come right back here.
- The first-start card names the AI it is preparing (it used to say GPT while Gemini was downloading).
- Screens in Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Japanese and Chinese no longer show leftover English text.
- On a PC that is almost out of memory, a failed sign-in now says so, and the name-detection models use about a third less memory.

### Sessions
- A Claude session set to **Auto** no longer quietly switches to **Edit** after you choose **Always allow**.
- When a GPT model is busy, thread0 says the model is busy and suggests trying again in a moment or picking another model, instead of showing an unknown error.
- GPT session names no longer show an internal language tag.
- Sessions that ran on a guest's subscription can be deleted.
- An error bar at the top goes away when you switch screens.
- Settings → Work has a **GPT memory** switch (off by default).
