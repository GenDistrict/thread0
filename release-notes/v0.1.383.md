# thread0 0.1.383

Windows and macOS (Apple Silicon) ship the same version.

A web preview that works like a browser and takes comments on the running app, new sessions that open instantly, every thread0 tool in GPT and Gemini sessions, and activity time for organizations.

## Changes

### Web preview
- Links to a development server in a chat (for example `http://localhost:5173/checkout`) now open in thread0's web preview at that page. Ctrl+click (⌘+click on Mac) still opens your browser.
- The preview has a browser bar: back, forward, reload, an address box, and **Reload without cache** under the menu. On a phone the address box gets its own line.
- The preview remembers the page you were on: reloading, closing and reopening, or restarting the development server brings you back to the same page.
- **Edit** now comments on the live app instead of a frozen picture. Switch between **Use app** (everything works as usual) and **Comment** (click an element, drag a box, or Alt+click a point). Comments stay pinned to their element while you scroll, and one list collects comments across pages. Ctrl+Shift+E (⌘+Shift+E) switches modes, even from inside the app.
- When you send, thread0 attaches one marked picture of the screen and, for each comment, the page address and which element it was on.

### Sessions
- **New session** (and Ctrl+N / ⌘+N) starts right away with the project's last settings — account, model, effort and permission. Shift+click, the **⋯** button or Ctrl+Shift+N (⌘+Shift+N) opens the full options. If the remembered settings are no longer allowed, the options open and explain why.
- When an account hits its usage limit and another account of the same AI is signed in, the limit card in the conversation offers **Continue with <account>**. With no other account it offers **Open account management**.
- Reopening a session your phone already had an older copy of shows the end of the conversation first.
- Deleting a Gemini session also deletes its Gemini conversation files.

### GPT and Gemini
- GPT and Gemini sessions now have the same thread0 tools as Claude: image, video, speech and transcription with your assigned media settings, registering result files, routine drafts, connectors and metered providers. This also works in older GPT conversations you reopen.
- Long thread0 tool calls (such as making a video) can run for up to an hour in every AI. Gemini used to stop them after 3 minutes.
- Updated the GPT engine (Codex 0.167.0) and the Gemini engine (1.5.3).
- A GPT account you just added and have not used yet shows **Connected** instead of **Not checked**. Adding the same account again is pointed out.

### Guests
- Cards that change your whole PC or account — connectors, metered providers, plugins — only go to you, never to a guest in a shared session.

### Copying in the PC app
- **Copy instructions** copies the marked picture together with the text on Windows and Mac. It also works inside an expanded preview.

### Organizations
- Organizations can turn on **activity time**: time per developer and work folder, with agent time and human time shown separately. It is off unless the organization enables it. Settings → Organization says what is sent, and **My active time** shows your own record.

### thread0 Remote
- When a saved PC is off, Remote says so within a few seconds and shows when it was last online.
