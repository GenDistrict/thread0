# thread0 0.1.389

Windows and macOS (Apple Silicon) both ship as 0.1.389.

Easier Claude sign-in, conversations that keep going when an account hits its limit, a steadier Remote on unstable networks, and newer GPT and Gemini engines.

## Changes

### Sign-in and accounts
- **Claude sign-in opens your browser** instead of a console window. If the browser does not open, the card offers to open the sign-in page again or paste a code, and **Sign in with the terminal** remains as a fallback.
- If Claude sign-in is not finished after two minutes, the first-run card also offers to start with GPT or Gemini, and a timeout says what was not finished.
- **When a conversation hits a usage limit**, it can continue on another signed-in account of the same AI, or move to an account of another AI.

### Remote (phone and browser)
- On a flapping connection, earlier messages keep loading, and attachments wait for the connection instead of failing.
- Chat attachments that were already fetched are not downloaded again, and only a failed piece is requested again.
- Interrupted result downloads resume where they stopped, and a file you already saved opens without waiting for the PC.
- After the phone has been asleep for a while, Remote reconnects instead of hanging.
- The composer no longer shows how old the model list is; that is in the model control's tooltip.
- Dictation keeps the screen on while it listens.

### Engines
- **GPT** now runs on Codex 0.170.0. When GPT hides or blocks an answer, the chat shows a short notice in its place. New GPT account errors (sign-in expired, a different ChatGPT workspace) show the right recovery card.
- **Gemini** now runs on Antigravity 1.5.11.

### More
- HTML results open as the live page in the released app.
- Updates work on networks that inspect HTTPS (the app also trusts the certificates installed on the PC).
- Settings → Help has a **User guide** link.
- A short in-app survey on the home screen: how you heard about thread0, and later why you use it and what is missing. Each question can be skipped and is asked once.
- Stable updates download from a faster download host.
