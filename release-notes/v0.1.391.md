# thread0 0.1.391

Windows and macOS (Apple Silicon) both ship as 0.1.391.

A smoother Remote, one viewer for reviewing and editing results — now including video — and team policies for organizations.

## Changes

### Remote (phone and browser)
- **Remote no longer stalls while the PC app saves.** On PCs with long result histories, every save could pause the PC app for a few seconds, and Remote froze with it. Results are now stored separately and saves take a few milliseconds.

### Reviewing and editing results
- **PDF results read like a normal PDF viewer**: all pages scroll as one document, pinch or Ctrl+wheel zooms, and text can be selected and copied. Select text and press **Comment**, or use **Mark an area**, then **Edit now** to send it to the session. Every PDF — results, files from the file list, and saved copies — opens in this viewer.
- **Images and HTML open in the same viewer** with no View/Edit switch: look around, mark or comment in place, and the instruction list appears once there is something to send.
- **Video results can be edited the same way.** While the video plays, comment on the current moment, mark a range from start to end, or pick a shape to pause and mark an area of the frame. **Edit now** sends the times and ranges along with the frames at the marked times.
- Long file paths and the **Copy instructions** / **Edit now** buttons no longer spill out of the panel at large text sizes.
- On iPhone Safari, a first long-press on a PDF selects text instead of the whole page, and selected text no longer looks doubled.

### Organizations
- **Team policies apply on the PC**: the organization policy and every team policy you belong to are combined, and the strictest setting wins in each area. A locked item shows which team set it.
- The organization intro reflects the new pricing: US$10 per person per month, with a free pilot available by application.
- Password-reset and email-verification mail for the admin console returns you to the console.
