# thread0 0.1.397

Windows and macOS (Apple Silicon) both ship as 0.1.397.

A new home that directs your sessions for you, sessions that can hand work to each other, organization content rules, and stronger security mode.

## Changes

### A new home
- **Home is one conversation.** The top line shows your AI accounts with their limits, and chips for connected devices and guests. Below it is a conversation of what you asked and a card for each session, and the message box stays at the bottom. The status tab is gone, and the session list groups sessions by state (needs you, working, results to check).
- **Results and approvals finish on home.** A card shows a session's progress, its full answer, files and approvals. You can approve, reply or open the session right from the card.
- **Type without picking a session and thread0 sends it where it belongs** — to the session already working on that subject, to a new session, or it answers you directly (for example "what is each session doing?"). When two sessions fit equally it asks with buttons. Instructions it sends appear on a card first and can be cancelled for a moment. It never changes files itself; the work always happens in a session.
- Pick a session with **@** to send to it directly. You can turn home routing off in Settings → Work.
- When an account reaches its usage limit, home asks which account should continue.
- Results seen on a home card count as checked.

### Sessions working together
- **A session can hand work to another session** when you ask — for example, develop in one session and have another review the code, then get the review back in the first session. A handed-over task cannot be handed on again.
- **Call another session with @ inside a session.** The @ list shows your other sessions first.
- New sessions get a short name for their work after the first turn, so they are easy to tell apart and to call.

### Organizations
- **Content rules**: organization and team rules (terms, restricted patterns, and built-in detection of identifiers, secrets and amounts) are checked on the PC before a message or its text attachments go to an AI. A rule can block, require a reason, mask the match, or only count it.
- Security turns forced by the organization no longer show a warning chip.

### Security mode
- **Tools that reach outside the PC ask before sending hidden values.** When a web search, a web page, media generation or a connector would receive masked names or details, a card asks whether to send the real values once, always in this session, keep the placeholders, or not send. The conversation itself keeps the placeholders.
- On Windows, Claude's PowerShell tool now works with masked values like the Bash tool: placeholders become the real values on your PC, and the output is masked before the AI sees it.
- The AI treats placeholders as the real people and organizations they stand for, instead of refusing to answer about them.
- Names from a session that uses the security check no longer leave through home routing or a hand-over to another session.
- The security checks are out of beta.

### Fixes and smaller changes
- An AI account whose login has really expired now shows as signed out, with a way to sign in again.
- Live comments on pages that show several scenes at one address (slides, animations) stay with their scene.
- Comment numbers follow the order they were added.
- Fable 5.2 costs are estimated at its own price.
